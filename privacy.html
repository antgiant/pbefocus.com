Privacy policy

PBEfocus.com · Last updated October 23, 2026

The short version: you choose where your data is kept: only on your device, or in your own Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive. Only that storage and the people you share it with can see your data. We run no database and no accounts, so we never receive it. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

Who we are

PBEfocus.com is a free set of tools for Pathfinder Bible Experience (PBE) families, coaches, and teams: Karaoke, Quiz Builder, Score Keeper, and others listed on the home page. It is made by volunteers, not by any church, conference, or Pathfinder organization, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. This policy covers the apps on pbefocus.com and its subdomains. If an app on another address links here, it is covered too.

What the apps handle

Whatever you put into them:

  • Karaoke: your playlists, genre mixes, study settings, and practice history. Your library link, if you use one, stays on your device.
  • Quiz Builder: people’s names you enter for profiles, their settings, and the question selections you make.
  • Score Keeper: team names, question scores, and session history.

If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, the app also shows your account name or email address so you can see who is signed in.

Where your data is kept

Each app keeps your data in this browser on this device by default. You can also choose to back it up and sync it. Who can see it depends on that choice:

  • Local only (your device). Your data stays inside this browser. It is not sent anywhere, and no one else can see it: not Google, not Microsoft, and not us. Clearing the site’s data in your browser erases it, so use an app’s export option if you want a backup.
  • Google Drive. Your data is saved in a folder named “PBE Focus” in your own Google Drive, so Google stores it and can see it, under Google’s own privacy terms.
  • Microsoft OneDrive. Your data is saved in the “Apps/PBE Focus” folder in your own OneDrive, so Microsoft stores it and can see it, under Microsoft’s own privacy terms.

All PBE apps use the same “PBE Focus” folder, so they can see each other’s data in your account. Each app keeps its own subfolder.

Sharing with other people

Some apps let you share a set of data (for example a group of Karaoke playlists, or an event in Score Keeper) with other people. When you share, the app puts that data in a separate shared folder and asks Google or OneDrive to share that folder with the people you choose. Everyone with access can see what is in that folder, and people you give edit access to can change it. Your own private data, such as practice history and device settings, is not put in a shared folder. You decide who to share with; the sharing is done by Google or Microsoft, not by us. You can stop sharing at any time in Google Drive or OneDrive, and anyone you shared with can leave a shared folder at any time in the app.

Who can see your data

Only the storage you chose (see above) and anyone you share it with. We cannot see it. The apps talk directly between your browser and your chosen storage; nothing passes through our servers, because we have none. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything other than showing it to you.

One more thing to know: the apps are delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub can see ordinary web request details (such as your IP address and browser type) when a page loads. That never includes your data.

Google permission

If you choose Google Drive, signing in asks Google for permission to see and manage only the files and folders the app creates or that you choose with Google’s file picker. It does not ask to see the rest of your Google Drive. We never see your Google password; you enter it on Google’s own sign-in page. Loading Google’s sign-in also lets Google see ordinary web request details, such as your IP address.

PBEfocus.com’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The information is used only to provide the features you see in the apps. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by people.

Microsoft permission

If you choose Microsoft OneDrive, signing in asks Microsoft for permission to read and write the app’s own folder in your OneDrive. If you later share data with other people or join something they shared, the app asks for a broader permission, which lets it read and write files in your OneDrive. Microsoft only offers that permission in one broad form. The app only opens the shared folder you choose and its own folder. If you use a work or school account, your organization may need to approve that broader permission. We never see your Microsoft password; you enter it on Microsoft’s own sign-in page.

Children’s information

Pathfinder teams include children, and what you enter may include their names and scores. We do not collect, see, or use them; they stay in your own storage, or in a shared folder you choose to share. The apps are intended for parents, guardians, coaches, and leaders, not for children to create accounts or enter their own information. Please share only with people you trust to see it.

Cookies and tracking

The apps use no analytics or advertising tools. They save your settings (such as your chosen storage) in your browser’s storage. They also use two small cookies shared across pbefocus.com:

  • A sign-in hint. After you connect Google Drive or OneDrive, a cookie remembers which service and which account email you used, so signing in on one PBE app signs you in on the others, and signing out signs you out of all of them. It contains no password and no access token.
  • A display setting. One cookie remembers whether you turned on a small cosmetic feature on the site.

Google’s and Microsoft’s sign-in pages may use their own cookies.

Your choices

  • Sign out on any PBE app. This disconnects cloud storage on all PBE apps in this browser and removes the sign-in hint. Your data stays on your device and in your cloud folder.
  • Delete your data by deleting the “PBE Focus” folder in Google Drive or OneDrive, or, for local data, by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • Leave or stop a share in the app (to leave) or in Google Drive or OneDrive (to stop sharing).
  • Remove the apps’ access any time in your Google Account’s third-party access settings or your Microsoft account’s app permissions.

Questions

Ask in the PBEfocus.com discussions on GitHub. Please do not post personal information there.
